# SIGNIX product demonstration

SIGNIX: “Sig-nix”. Microsoft Zira synthetic English narration.

## opening — 18 seconds

Before investing in solar, one decision connects many questions.

Is the roof suitable? What could it generate? And how might that investment perform?

Signix brings those questions into one rooftop planning workspace.

## home — 8 seconds

Begin with a dedicated Home planning experience, using the location and electricity inputs for your scenario.

## mapping — 22 seconds

The satellite map provides visual context. Draw a planning boundary, then mark the areas you want to exclude.

Here, a demonstration footprint and obstruction show how usable area changes before sizing begins.

This is an illustrative planning exercise, not a survey of this building.

## planner — 15 seconds

The rooftop designer connects the footprint to a panel layout preview.

Switch between views and inspect the arrangement. Prototype geometry and unverified shade remain clearly identified.

## energy — 22 seconds

Generation forecasts turn the selected system into monthly and long term energy estimates.

The application exposes its data source and planning assumptions.

This demonstration uses mock weather fallback. These figures illustrate the calculation workflow, rather than verified site production.

## finance — 19 seconds

For a home, review the estimated investment, household savings, and payback alongside the assumptions behind them.

Any residential assistance is indicative and subject to eligibility and official verification.

A planning estimate is never a guarantee of savings.

## business — 25 seconds

Business planning uses its own commercial policies while sharing the rooftop and energy workflow.

Review capital cost, the annual operations and maintenance reserve, net operating benefit, return on investment, and payback.

Residential subsidy is not a commercial benefit. Demand charge savings are not modelled.

## compare — 20 seconds

Save a configuration, change one planning choice, and compare the resulting scenarios for the same roof.

The side by side view makes differences in capacity, investment, and expected performance easier to discuss.

The calculation engine supplies the results shown here.

## pdf — 15 seconds

Export the planning summary for a conversation with an installer or project partner.

Keep the assumptions and limitations with the figures, so the next decision starts with the same context.

## bilingual — 10 seconds

The same report can be explored in English and Hindi, helping more people participate in the decision.

## closing — 19 seconds

Map the roof. Understand the energy potential. Compare the investment.

Signix. Rooftop Solar Planning.

Use the estimate to ask better questions, then verify the site, design, and approvals before procurement.
